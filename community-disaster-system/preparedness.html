Disaster Preparedness

Click a disaster type to expand its preparedness checklist. Knowing what to do before disaster strikes is the single best way to stay safe.

Flood Preparedness
  • Move to higher ground
  • Keep emergency supplies ready
  • Avoid walking or driving through floodwater
  • Switch off electricity if instructed
  • Follow official emergency alerts
Earthquake Preparedness
  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On
  • Stay away from windows
  • Do not use elevators
  • Keep an emergency kit
  • Identify safe places in your home
Fire Preparedness
  • Know emergency exits
  • Keep fire extinguishers ready
  • Do not use elevators during a fire
  • Stay low in smoke
  • Call emergency services
Cyclone / Severe Weather Preparedness
  • Stay indoors
  • Secure doors and windows
  • Keep food, water, and medicines ready
  • Charge phones and power banks
  • Follow weather warnings
Landslide Preparedness
  • Avoid dangerous slopes during heavy rainfall
  • Stay alert for unusual sounds
  • Move away from affected areas
  • Follow evacuation instructions
