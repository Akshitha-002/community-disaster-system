Safety Guides

General guidance for what to do before, during, and after a disaster.

Before a Disaster

  • Prepare an emergency kit
  • Know your evacuation routes
  • Save emergency numbers
  • Stay informed on local alerts

During a Disaster

  • Stay calm and follow instructions
  • Move to safety immediately
  • Avoid damaged structures and floodwater
  • Help others if it's safe to do so

After a Disaster

  • Check for injuries and get medical help
  • Avoid re-entering damaged buildings
  • Document damage for records
  • Follow official recovery guidance
